# Game design source of truth

## One-sentence pitch
Collect little animals by playing with your friends. Take care of them at home. Then bring them
back out to play games together.

## Pillars
1. **Persistent attachment** — your pet exists between matches and feels like yours.
2. **Social discovery** — memorable eggs come from doing things together.
3. **Physical comedy** — multiplayer uses simple, chunky, readable physics.
4. **Low punishment** — care is affectionate, not guilt-driven.
5. **Procedural surprise** — hatching should create “look at this little freak” moments, not optimization spreadsheets.

## Hub
The player uses their normal Roblox avatar. The selected pet follows them. The first hub is a small
backyard/playroom with food, washing, toys, petting, a hatch nest, a hidden egg, and the couch area.

Visible needs: **Food · Clean · Happy**.
Needs may make a pet sleepy/grumpy/dirty but cannot kill, delete, or permanently weaken it.

## Eggs
Primary acquisition is activity/discovery, not a gacha machine:
- Backyard Egg — exploration/secret reward.
- Play Egg — activity reward.
- Party Egg — party-game reward.

## Procedural pets
Pets are assembled from independent components:
head, ears, body, legs, tail, pattern, palette.

Initial catalogs stay intentionally small. Body parts may create small physical traits:
- chunky body: slightly harder to shove, slightly slower;
- tiny body: slightly faster, easier to launch;
- long legs: slightly higher jump;
- frog legs: stronger charged jump;
- otter tail: improved swimming.

Rarity never gives raw power.

## Friendship
Care/play builds friendship and unlocks tricks, emotes, victory dances, sleeping poses, and cosmetics.
Friendship does not increase competitive power.

## Universal party controls
move · jump · grab · carry · shove · throw · flop · get up

## First party mode: King of the Couch
Stay on the couch. Knock everyone else off. Short rounds, immediate replay, readable win state.

Follow-up: Big Haul, where players cooperatively move one absurdly oversized object through a short obstacle path.
